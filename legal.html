Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

CyberNexa Solutions ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Fees, Refunds & Course Terms

Last updated: October 2026

Enrolment: A seat is confirmed once your enrolment request is approved and the course fee (or the first agreed instalment) has been received. Requests are reviewed within one business day.

Fees: Course fees are listed on the courses page and may change for future batches. Your confirmed fee is locked for the batch you enrol in. Instalment plans are available on request and must be agreed in writing before classes begin.

Refunds: Cancel before your first class for a full refund. Cancel within the first 7 days (and before attending more than two classes) for a 50% refund. After that, fees are non-refundable, but we will offer a credit toward another batch where possible. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 14 business days.

Attendance & certificate: Certificates are issued to students who complete the course with at least 70% attendance and clear the required assessments. Missed classes can be made up in a later batch subject to seat availability.

Conduct: Students must respect instructors and classmates. We may suspend or remove any student for harassment, sharing course material commercially, or attempting to disrupt academy systems.
